This website is run by Aashish Khadka, Pokhara, Nepal. This page explains what information is collected when you visit, and why.

## What I collect

- **Messages you send.** If you use the contact form or email me, I receive your name, email address and anything you choose to include. I use it only to reply to you.
- **Analytics.** I use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or track you across sites, to understand which pages are read.
- **Google Analytics.** I use Google Analytics to see how visitors find and use the site. Google may set cookies. You can opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Meta Pixel.** This site uses the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure visits from Meta ads and to show relevant ads. Meta may set cookies. You can control this in your Facebook ad settings and your browser.

## What I don't do

- I don't sell your information.
- I don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

## How long I keep it

Messages are kept for as long as needed to reply and for my records of client work. Ask me at any time to delete them.
