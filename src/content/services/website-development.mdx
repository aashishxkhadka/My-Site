## Websites built to bring in business

A website has one main job: **turn visitors into enquiries, bookings or sales.** Looking good matters, but a beautiful site that's slow, hard to find on Google or confusing to use doesn't help the business.

Every site we build is planned around that job: clear pages, fast loading on mobile, search-friendly structure from day one, and an easy path to get in touch.

## Fast, mobile-first and SEO-ready

Most visitors in Nepal arrive on a phone, often on a mobile connection. Sites are built to be:

- **Fast:** optimised images, minimal code and good Core Web Vitals scores
- **Mobile-first:** designed for small screens, then scaled up
- **Search-friendly:** clean URLs, proper headings, structured data and a sitemap, ready for [SEO](/services/seo/)
- **Accessible:** readable text, good contrast and keyboard-friendly navigation

## Website migration without losing rankings

Moving from an old WordPress, Wix or builder site is a common reason sites lose traffic. A careful migration includes:

1. A list of every URL Google has indexed
2. A redirect plan mapping old URLs to new pages
3. Preserving the content that already ranks
4. Checking Google Search Console after launch and fixing anything that slips
