## What SEO actually does for a business

Search engine optimisation (SEO) is the work of making sure that **when someone searches for what you offer, they find you**, not a competitor. Done well, it brings in customers who are already looking, month after month, without paying for every click.

For most businesses, that means three things working together:

1. **Technical SEO:** search engines can crawl, render and understand every important page.
2. **Content:** your pages clearly answer the questions your customers are asking.
3. **Trust:** other sites, reviews and consistent business details show that you are real and reputable.

## Technical SEO

Technical problems are invisible to visitors but can quietly stop a site from ranking. A technical audit covers:

- Crawling and indexing: pages blocked by mistake, duplicate pages, broken links and redirects
- Site speed and Core Web Vitals on mobile
- Site structure and internal linking, so authority flows to the pages that matter
- Structured data (schema) for your business, services, articles and FAQs
- Migrations: moving to a new platform or domain without losing rankings

I fix issues in order of impact, so the work that matters most happens first.

## Local SEO in Pokhara and across Nepal

If your customers are nearby, **Google Maps is often more important than your website.** Local SEO focuses on:

- A complete, accurate **Google Business Profile** with the right categories, services, photos and posts
- **Consistent name, address and phone (NAP)** details across directories and social profiles
- A simple, honest process for asking happy customers for **reviews**
- Location pages and local content that match how people search, such as "dentist in Lakeside Pokhara"

## SEO for AI search (GEO)

People increasingly ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews instead of scrolling through ten blue links. These tools quote sources that are **clear, specific and trustworthy**. Generative engine optimisation (GEO) builds on good SEO with:

- Direct, quotable answers near the top of important pages
- Clear entity information: who you are, what you do, where you are
- Structured data that links your business, people and services together
- Content that covers a topic properly rather than a thin page per keyword

## SEO vs. paid ads

| | SEO | Paid ads |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | Builds over months | Traffic from day one |
| **Cost over time** | Compounds; no cost per click | Stops when you stop paying |
| **Trust** | Organic results earn more clicks | Clearly marked as ads |
| **Best for** | Long-term, steady demand | Launches, offers and testing |

Most businesses benefit from both. If you need results quickly while SEO builds, pair it with [digital marketing](/services/digital-marketing/).
