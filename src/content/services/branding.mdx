## Why branding matters for growth

People decide quickly whether a business looks trustworthy. A clear, consistent brand does a lot of quiet work: it makes you **recognisable**, makes you look as professional as you are, and makes every rupee spent on marketing go further, because people remember who it came from.

Branding isn't decoration. It's the part of your [growth strategy](/services/growth-strategy/) that people can see.

## Brand strategy comes first

Before any design, we answer the questions that shape everything else:

- **Who is the brand for?** The customers you most want to win
- **What do you stand for?** The one or two ideas you want to be known for
- **How are you different?** Why a customer should choose you over the alternatives
- **How do you sound?** Formal or friendly, expert or approachable

These answers become a short brand strategy document, so design decisions are judged against something real rather than personal taste.

## What's included in a brand identity

| Element | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Logo** | The signature of the business, designed to work small, large, in colour and in one colour |
| **Colour palette** | Primary and supporting colours, checked for readability on screens and print |
| **Typography** | Fonts for headings and text, chosen to be legible and distinctive |
| **Graphic elements** | Patterns, icons and photo style that make your materials recognisable at a glance |
| **Voice** | Words and tone for captions, ads, emails and web copy |
| **Guidelines** | One document that keeps everyone consistent |

## Rebranding without losing what works

If you already have customers who know you, a full rebrand can do more harm than good. A **brand refresh** keeps the recognisable parts, such as a colour, a symbol or a name, and modernises the rest. It's often the smarter choice, and we'll decide together after looking at what you have.

## Branding for businesses in Nepal and abroad

I work with businesses in Pokhara, across Nepal and abroad. For local businesses, the brand has to work on a shop sign, a Facebook profile picture and a WhatsApp chat just as well as on a website. For international brands, it has to travel across markets. Both are planned from the start.
