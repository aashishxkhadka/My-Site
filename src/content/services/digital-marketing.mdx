## Digital marketing that starts with the business

Plenty of businesses are "doing digital marketing": posting regularly, boosting the odd post, running ads when sales are slow. What's often missing is the thread connecting all of it to **real customers**.

I start with your goals, then choose channels, content and budget to match. Every campaign has a clear purpose and a number attached to it.

## Social media marketing

Social media works best when it's consistent, useful and recognisably yours. I help with:

- **A content plan:** themes, formats and a realistic posting rhythm your team can keep up
- **Creative direction:** how posts, reels and stories should look and sound, in line with [your brand](/services/branding/)
- **Community:** replying to comments and messages quickly, because on Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp the conversation *is* the sale

## Paid advertising: Meta and Google Ads

Paid campaigns are the fastest way to reach new people, and the fastest way to waste money without a plan.

| Platform | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Meta Ads** (Facebook, Instagram) | Reaching people by interest and location, launches, offers, retargeting |
| **Google Search Ads** | Capturing people actively searching for what you sell |
| **Google Performance Max / Maps** | Local businesses that want calls, directions and bookings |

Every campaign gets clear targeting, a single goal, and a landing page or message flow built to convert.

## Tracking and reporting

If you can't measure it, you can't improve it. Before campaigns launch I set up:

- The **Meta Pixel** and conversion events
- **Google Analytics** and Google Ads conversion tracking
- Tracking for the actions that matter to you: forms, calls, WhatsApp clicks or purchases

Monthly reports explain what happened in plain language: what was spent, what it brought in, and what we'll change next.

## Marketing for businesses in Nepal

Customers in Nepal often discover businesses on Facebook and Instagram, then decide over **Messenger, WhatsApp or a phone call**. Campaigns are designed around that reality, with quick replies and clear calls to action, rather than assuming every customer fills out a website form.

## Marketing and SEO work better together

Paid campaigns bring results quickly; [SEO](/services/seo/) builds traffic that keeps coming without paying per click. Running both, guided by one [growth strategy](/services/growth-strategy/), means each one makes the other stronger.
